Makkah

The Kaaba,
the still centre.

Everything in Makkah turns toward one point — Masjid al-Haram and the Kaaba at its heart, where pilgrims circle in Tawaf. We arrange hotels within walking distance of the Haram, so the rhythm of your day can stay close to it, unrushed.

Madinah

The Green Dome,
a quieter pace.

After Makkah, most journeys settle into Madinah's calm — Masjid an-Nabawi and its Green Dome, gardens, and a gentler rhythm of prayer. We time your stay so Madinah feels like rest, not another leg of travel.
